Daily Discovery

A Little Learning, Every Day!

Daily Discovery is a free resource I created for my own homeschoolers to encourage curiosity, independent learning, and exploration.

Each day, students can discover bite-sized pieces of interesting and educational content covering history, vocabulary, poetry, science, and more!

What You'll Find

On This Day in History – Explore historical events with Encyclopaedia Britannica.

Word of the Day – Discover new vocabulary with Vocabulary.com.

Poem-a-Day – Explore poetry through the Academy of American Poets.

Space & Earth – Discover something new with NASA's Astronomy Picture of the Day.

Science Discovery – Read interesting science articles from Science News Explores.

How to Use Daily Discovery

Simply open the page, grab a notebook or journal, and follow the daily prompts. Each section links to a free educational resource.

Daily Discovery is designed to be a simple addition to your homeschool routine, encouraging students to learn something new every day without requiring an extensive lesson plan.
